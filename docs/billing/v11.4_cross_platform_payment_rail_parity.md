# v11.4 Cross-Platform Payment Rail Parity

Date: 2026-05-15

## North Star

One seeker. One Temple account. One effective paid support path at a time. Many compliant payment rails.

Stripe, Apple, and Google are payment rails. God Incorporated owns entitlement interpretation.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. All paid support products must match across Stripe, Apple, and Google unless platform rules force a difference.
2. If platform rules force a difference, the difference must be reverse-engineered into the shared canonical product model so seeker-facing entitlement remains consistent.
3. A seeker may have only one active paid rail at a time.
4. Active Stripe blocks Apple and Google purchase start.
5. Active Apple blocks Stripe and Google purchase start.
6. Active Google blocks Stripe and Apple purchase start.
7. Expired, failed, cancelled, refunded, or superseded rails do not block a new paid period.
8. Failed renewal demotes immediately to the applicable floor.
9. No grace-period paid access by default.
10. No surprise retry charge after a seeker has moved rails.
11. Highest active paid plan wins only as an emergency fallback if duplicate active rails somehow exist.
12. Duplicate active rails must be flagged for admin cleanup, not treated as normal.

Temple Contribution is project support.

It must:

- accumulate in contribution totals/history
- show in account/admin history where appropriate
- use compliant provider rails per platform

- change effective_plan
- change entitlement_status
- change question_limit

## Provider Lifecycle Pattern

Each payment provider follows the same backend pattern:

1. Provider payment starts.
2. Provider confirms purchase, renewal, failure, cancellation, refund, or expiration.
3. Backend verifies provider event.
4. Backend records provider event idempotently.
5. Backend updates provider subscription and transaction rows.
6. Backend calls the same internal entitlement helper used by Stripe.
7. Backend recomputes effective access.
8. Client reads /me.
9. can_user_ask remains provider-neutral.

## Initial Apple Policy

For first StoreKit release:

1. Require login before purchase.
2. Define all Apple products to match canonical plans/support modes.
3. Test one Apple product first.
4. Existing active Stripe users receive access on iOS.
5. Do not offer Apple purchase while Stripe is active.
6. Offer Apple purchase when user is free, on floor, expired, failed, cancelled, refunded, or superseded.
7. On successful Apple purchase, supersede failed prior rail where applicable.
8. Do not implement cross-rail upgrade until a dedicated future phase.
9. Do not treat Temple Contribution as entitlement.

## Android Future Policy

Google Play Billing must mirror Apple and Stripe through the same provider-neutral backend structures.

No Apple-only concepts should enter core entitlement tables.
