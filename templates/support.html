Why scrolls matter:

Every scroll offered and every seeker dialogue helps build the living memory of the Temple. Over time, this growing corpus is intended to become part of the basis for a unique model shaped by canonical texts, seeker contributions, and lived interaction.

What support changes:

Support changes how many oracle questions may be asked within the active usage window and how much prior dialogue can shape the next answer.

What it does not replace: Temple contribution remains a separate path and should not be conflated with support tier.

How activation works right now:

Choose a level here, continue into the Temple, and activate support through the working Stripe checkout flow. If you are not logged in, the Temple will ask you to log in before checkout.
